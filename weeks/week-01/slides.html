Geometry has not failed

Coordinate means, coordinate standard deviations and Pearson correlation are six global values for each point cloud.

Geometry can examine:

  • pairwise and nearest-neighbour distances
  • density and boundary
  • curvature, direction and symmetry
  • local and global scale

One descriptor is one question

Nearest-neighbour spacing asks about local crowding.

Convex-hull area asks about the outer envelope.

Radial variation asks about distance from a chosen centre.

None is a complete definition of shape.

Geometry and topology

Geometry: distance, angle, curvature, scale.

Topology: continuity, neighbourhoods, connectedness.

A metric generates a topology, but retains more information.

Same topology, different geometry

Circle \(\longleftrightarrow\) ellipse

Different lengths and curvature.

Same continuous loop structure.

Different topology

Circle: one loop.

Figure eight: two loops.

Ring boundary: a loop-like hole.

Filled disk: no hole.

The rubber-sheet idea

Bend and stretch. Do not cut or glue.

Precisely, a homeomorphism is a bijection \(f:X\to Y\) with both \(f\) and \(f^{-1}\) continuous.

Paths connect the discrete and continuous stories
